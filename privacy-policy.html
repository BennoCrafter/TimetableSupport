Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This platform does not collect, process, store, or share any data about its users.

If you have questions about our privacy policy, need assistance, or wish to report any privacy-related concerns, please contact us via email at timetablemanager.contact@gmail.com.

By reaching out to us, you may provide us with personal information, such as your email address or any details included in your inquiry. This information will be used solely to respond to your query and will not be shared with third parties.

When submitting feedback through our feedback form, the title and description of your feedback will be automatically created as a GitHub issue in our repository. This helps us track and address your concerns more effectively. The information you provide will be visible in our GitHub repository, so please ensure you don't include sensitive personal information in your feedback. We use this system to maintain transparency and keep track of user suggestions and concerns.

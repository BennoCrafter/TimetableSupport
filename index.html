Setup

Ready before the first bell.

Tick your subjects in a ready-made list, already named and colored. Then set the times for each subject once. A classmate already has theirs? Import their file and you're set in seconds.

Tasks & groups

One person writes it down. Everyone has it.

Homework and tests, grouped by when they're due. Start a group with your friends and share them via iCloud: someone adds the maths homework, and it shows up on everyone's device.

Grades

Know where you stand.

Weighting, subject and overall averages, and a trend chart. Scan the test you got back and keep it right next to the grade.

Themes

Make it yours.

Pick one of the built-in themes or design your own.

Widgets

Your next class, right on the Home Screen.

Widgets for your day, your next class, your tasks and your grades.

iPad

Your whole week on one screen.

A freeform dashboard: arrange timetable, tasks and grades however you like.

Also included: School holidays for your region, added automatically

Why it's free

Timetable Manager is an indie app. I spent ages looking for the perfect timetable app myself, but everywhere I went, pro features locked behind a paywall got on my nerves. So over the last three years, I built my own.

If you enjoy the app, I'd be happy about a coffee on Ko-fi. It helps me keep improving it.

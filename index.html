Everything for your school day

Timetable Creator

Build your timetable step by step: pick a subject, set its times, and it repeats every week on its own.

Day and week view

Today's classes with periods and breaks at a glance. Swipe to any week, and one tap takes you back to today.

Built for iPad

A freeform dashboard: put your timetable, tasks and grades wherever you want, and stack cards into tabs.

Homework and tests

Add homework and tests right to a class. They're due at the next lesson by default, with a reminder so nothing slips.

Groups for your class

Share homework and tests with classmates through iCloud. When one of you adds something, everyone has it.

Grades and averages

Weighted grades, subject and overall averages, and a trend chart. Link a grade to its test and keep a scan of the paper.

Holidays included

Public holidays and school breaks for your region show up in your timetable automatically.

Widgets

Today's schedule, open tasks, grades, upcoming holidays and countdowns, right on your Home Screen.

Themes and app icons

Pick one of the built-in themes or design your own, and choose the app icon you like best.
